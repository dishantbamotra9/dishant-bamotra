Sheet 02 — About

Mechanical Design Engineer with hands-on manufacturing and quality experience.

I don't only build CAD models — I understand how components are manufactured, fabricated, inspected and assembled.

My background is in manufacturing, quality and fabrication, with hands-on exposure to sheet metal, modular furniture manufacturing, inspection and production processes. I'm now applying that shop-floor perspective to mechanical design and CAD work, using SolidWorks and AutoCAD to turn requirements into models and drawings that hold up on the production floor, not just on screen.

01

Practical, not theoretical

Designs are shaped by what's actually feasible to fabricate, machine or form.

02

Manufacturable by default

Tolerances, fits and bend allowances are considered from the first sketch.

03

Assembly & inspection-friendly

Quality-engineering background means designs anticipate how they'll be checked and assembled, not just how they'll be modeled.

04

Cost-conscious

Material and process choices are weighed against production cost, not chosen for their own sake.

Sheet 03 — Services

Services I Offer

Engagements can run end-to-end or cover a single step — model only, drawings only, or a DXF export for laser cutting.

01

Mechanical Design

Concept-to-detail design of parts and assemblies for manufacturing, driven by your requirement or sketch.

Best for: new product ideas, custom components, machine parts.

Delivers: 3D model, manufacturing drawing, revision support.

Example: Plummer Block Assembly

02

3D CAD Modeling

Parametric part and assembly modeling in SolidWorks, built for easy revision and downstream drawing work.

Best for: product developers, machine builders, engineering teams.

Delivers: SLDPRT / SLDASM, STEP export.

Example: Planetary Gear Assembly

03

Sheet Metal Design

Enclosures, brackets and formed parts designed with real bend allowances, reliefs and press-brake limits in mind.

Best for: electrical enclosures, panels, brackets, fabrication shops.

Delivers: 3D model, flat pattern, DXF, bend table.

Example: Sheet Metal Electrical Enclosure

04

Weldment Design

Structural frames and welded assemblies using standard structural members, with weld callouts and cut lists.

Best for: frames, stands, structural fabrication.

Delivers: weldment model, cut-list BOM, fabrication drawing.

Example: AutoCAD Weldment Drawing

05

Manufacturing Drawings

Clear, correctly toleranced 2D drawings that a machine shop or fabricator can quote and build from without follow-up calls.

Best for: anyone needing shop-ready documentation.

Delivers: dimensioned drawing (PDF / DWG), title block, revision table.

Example: Gear Train Drawing Set

06

2D Drafting

AutoCAD drafting with correct layer standards, line weights and GD&T for manufacturing documentation.

Best for: shops standardizing on AutoCAD, layout drawings.

Delivers: DWG / PDF, layered per standard.

Example: AutoCAD Manufacturing Drawings

07

Assembly Design

Multi-part assemblies built with correct mates, motion and load paths — checked for interference before release.

Best for: mechanisms, fixtures, tooling assemblies.

Delivers: assembly model, exploded view, assembly drawing.

Example: Bench Vice Assembly

08

BOM Preparation

Accurate bills of material pulled directly from the CAD assembly, ready for purchasing or quoting.

Best for: procurement-ready project handoff.

Delivers: BOM (Excel / PDF) linked to drawing item numbers.

Example: Gear Assembly BOM

09

DXF for Laser Cutting

Flat-pattern DXF files exported directly from sheet-metal models, ready for laser or plasma cutting.

Best for: laser-cutting shops, sheet-metal fabricators.

Delivers: DXF flat pattern, bend-line layer.

Example: Enclosure Flat Pattern

10

Reverse Engineering

Rebuilding a CAD model and drawing from an existing physical part, sample or reference image.

Best for: legacy parts with no digital record.

Delivers: 3D model, manufacturing drawing.

Example: discuss your part's measurements or sample.

11

Design Modification

Updating an existing model or drawing for a new requirement, tolerance fix or manufacturing feedback.

Best for: iterating on a part that's already in production.

Delivers: revised model and drawing with change log.

Example: send your current file and the change needed.

12

DFM Review

Design-for-manufacturing review of an existing design, flagging tolerance, fit or process issues before it goes to the shop.

Best for: catching costly issues before production.

Delivers: annotated review notes and suggested changes.

Example: send your model or drawing for review.
