Sheet Metal Electrical Enclosure

A sheet-metal enclosure for electrical control-panel components, engineered with bend reliefs, ventilation and an access panel — carried through to a laser-ready flat pattern.

CategorySheet Metal / Enclosure Design
SoftwareSolidWorks Sheet Metal
SkillsFlat Pattern, Drawing Module
LevelAdvanced
DeliverablesModel, Flat Pattern, DXF, A3 Drawing

Isometric Render — Sheet Metal Enclosure

Requirement

Design a sheet-metal enclosure to house electrical control-panel components, with adequate ventilation, an access panel for servicing, and geometry that folds cleanly on a press brake.

Design Approach

The enclosure was modeled directly as a SolidWorks sheet-metal part so bends, reliefs and the flat pattern stay linked to the 3D model. Ventilation louvers were placed on a side panel and sized to balance airflow against structural stiffness of the panel.

Key Design Features

  • 1 mm stainless-steel sheet, press-brake formed
  • Ventilation louvers punched prior to forming
  • Hinged access door for internal servicing
  • Powder-coated finish specified after fabrication

Manufacturing Considerations

  • Bend radius held at R0.2 across all three primary bends for consistent tooling
  • Bend reliefs sized to prevent tearing at panel corners
  • Flat pattern exported directly to DXF for laser cutting, with a separate bend-line layer

Assembly Considerations

The enclosure body and door were modeled as separate sheet-metal parts so the door could be hinged and opened independently, with fastener locations coordinated between both parts.

Material

1 mm SS-316 sheet, press-brake formed, powder-coated finish.

A3 Manufacturing Drawing — Electrical Enclosure

Bend Table

  • Bend 1 — 90° · R0.2 · Up
  • Bend 2 — 90° · R0.2 · Down
  • Bend 3 — 90° · R0.2 · Up
Final Result

A manufacturing-ready enclosure with a verified flat pattern, bend-relief detailing and a DXF export suitable for laser cutting — demonstrating the full sheet-metal-to-fabrication pipeline.
