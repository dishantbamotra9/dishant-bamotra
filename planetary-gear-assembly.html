Planetary Gear Assembly

A compact speed-reduction gear train — sun, planet and ring gears meshing with equal load-sharing across three planet gears. Built to demonstrate correct gear geometry, motion validation and full manufacturing documentation.

CategoryGear System / Power Transmission
SoftwareSolidWorks, GearTrax
SkillsGear Mates, Motion Study
LevelAdvanced
DeliverablesModel, Assembly, A3 Drawing, BOM

Isometric Render — Planetary Gear Assembly

Requirement

Develop a planetary gear train capable of compact speed reduction, with equal load-sharing across three planet gears and correct meshing between the sun, planet and ring gears.

Design Approach

Gear geometry was sized around a common module so the sun, planet and ring gears mesh correctly at the calculated center distances. Three planet gears were arranged symmetrically around the sun gear to share load evenly and keep the carrier balanced.

Key Design Features

  • Sun, planet and ring gear meshing geometry sized for correct module match
  • Center-distance calculations verified across all three gear pairs
  • Carrier designed to hold three planet gears in symmetric, balanced arrangement

CAD Modeling Process

Gears were generated using GearTrax for accurate involute tooth profiles, then assembled in SolidWorks with gear mates to capture the correct rotational relationship between sun, planet and ring gears.

Manufacturing Considerations

  • Alloy steel specified for all gears and carrier for wear resistance under load
  • Standard module and pressure angle chosen for manufacturability with common hobbing/shaping tooling
  • ISO 7040-M3 fasteners specified for carrier assembly

Assembly Considerations

A SolidWorks Motion Study was used to verify that the gear train rotates correctly and that the planet gears mesh without interference before the drawing was released.

A3 Manufacturing Drawing — Gear Train

Bill of Materials

  • Sun Gear — Qty 1 — Alloy Steel (Case-Hardened)
  • Planet Gear — Qty 3 — Alloy Steel (Case-Hardened)
  • Ring Gear (Internal) — Qty 1 — Alloy Steel
  • Carrier — Qty 1 — Alloy Steel
  • ISO 7040-M3 Fasteners — Qty 3

Gear Parameters

  • Module: 1 · Pressure Angle: 20°
  • Teeth — Ring / Planet / Sun: 60 / 24 / 12
  • Face Width: 10 mm
Final Result

A verified gear-train assembly with confirmed kinematics, torque distribution and motion transmission, released with a complete A3 manufacturing drawing, BOM and gear-parameter table.
