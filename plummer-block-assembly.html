Plummer Block Assembly

A pillow-block bearing housing engineered to support a rotating shaft, with correct bearing seating and housing alignment.

CategoryBearing Housing / Rotating Equipment
SoftwareSolidWorks
SkillsAssembly Mates, Bearing Fits
LevelIntermediate
DeliverablesModel, Assembly, Drawing

Isometric Render — Plummer Block Assembly

Requirement

Design a pillow-block (plummer block) bearing housing to support a rotating shaft, ensuring the bearing seats correctly and the housing halves align without play.

Design Approach

The housing was split at the bearing centerline into a base and a cap, allowing the bronze bush to be seated and secured without needing to slide it over the full shaft length — a common practical requirement for maintenance access.

Key Design Features

  • Housing split at bearing centerline for serviceability
  • Bronze bush/bearing seated in a ground bore
  • Grease-nipple provision for periodic lubrication

Manufacturing Considerations

  • Housing machined from a cast base; bore ground to H7 class for accurate bearing seating
  • Shaft fit held to H7/g6 for a controlled running clearance
  • Cast-iron housing and cap chosen for rigidity and vibration damping

Assembly Considerations

The cap and base are located with dowels and secured with through-bolts, keeping the split line aligned so the bearing bore stays round under clamping load.

Material

Housing and cap in cast iron; bush in phosphor bronze; fasteners in mild steel.

Bill of Materials

  • Housing (Base) — Qty 1 — Cast Iron
  • Bearing Cap — Qty 1 — Cast Iron
  • Bronze Bush / Bearing — Qty 1 — Phosphor Bronze
  • Cap — Qty 1 — Cast Iron
  • Bolt with Nuts — Qty 2 — Mild Steel
Final Result

A bearing-housing assembly with verified bore tolerances, shaft-alignment principles applied correctly, and a serviceable split-housing design suitable for rotating-equipment applications.
